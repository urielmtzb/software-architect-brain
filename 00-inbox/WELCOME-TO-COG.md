## Quick Start

### 1. Daily Morning Routine
Invoke the `daily-brief` skill to get your personalized intelligence briefing covering:
- Software & Enterprise Architecture Patterns
- Cloud-Native Engineering & Distributed Systems
- Enterprise Integration & Data Platforms
- Artificial Intelligence & Agentic Architectures

### 2. Capture Your Architectural Ideas
Use the `braindump` skill to quickly capture system design ideas, ADR notes, or technical evaluations. Your braindumps will automatically be categorized into your professional folders.

### 3. Conduct Architectural Research
Use `auto-research` or ask COG to evaluate technology trade-offs, compare frameworks (e.g., Kafka vs RabbitMQ, Modular Monolith vs Microservices), or synthesize architecture patterns.

### 4. Weekly Reflection
Every week, use the `weekly-checkin` skill to review your week's insights, decision records, and pattern evolution.
